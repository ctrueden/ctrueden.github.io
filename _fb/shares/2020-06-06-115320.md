Kickboxing and Shaolin Kempo Karate are awesome for so many reasons: body awareness; confidence; agility, grace and proficiency of movement; health and fitness; and of course self-defense skills. And unlike most sports, you get a great workout in place without special equipment.

Villari's Madison Martial Arts Cooperative is offering online training and just majorly cut prices during COVID-19—so if you have ever been interested in martial arts or cardio kickboxing for fitness, but money or location have been obstacles, now is a good time to give it a try!
