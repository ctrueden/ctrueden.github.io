I wrote a while ago about how insightful I have found [Paul Graham's articles](http://www.paulgraham.com/articles.html). Today (thanks to Slashdot comment surfing) I discovered another cache of insightful material: [writings about the Internet](http://www.shirky.com/) by Clay Shirky. I enjoyed reading his [objective](http://www.shirky.com/writings/riaa_encryption.html) [analyses](http://www.shirky.com/writings/file-sharing_social.html) of file sharing (he tells it like it is, rather than offering praise or condemnation for any party involved), as well as his [smackdown of the Semantic Web](http://www.shirky.com/writings/semantic_syllogism.html). [DNA, P2P, and Privacy](http://shirky.com/writings/privacy_p2p.html) looks intriguing as well.
