I'm sure everyone's already heard about the [controller for the Nintendo Revolution](<http://www.1up.com/do/newsStory?cId=3143782>). Personally, I think it will be awesome, as long as it can perform well. For those who haven't seen the [teaser video](<http://cube.ign.com/articles/651/651334p1.html>), I suggest watching it to get an idea of what Nintendo has in mind with this controller.  
  
Penny Arcade linked an [interesting article from Lost Garden](<http://lostgarden.com/2005/09/nintendos-genre-innovation-strategy.html>) a while back about Nintendo's genre innovation. While the article does oversimplify some things, it makes a lot of insightful points about Nintendo's strategy in the video game market.  
  
More recently, [David Sirlin](<http://sirlin.net>), a bastion of reason in an insane world, posted [his thoughts on the Revolution controller](<http://www.sirlin.net/.blog/2005/09/nintendo-revolution-controller.html>) as well, and as usual I strongly agree with his sentiments.
