**Protoman:** "Let's see how fancy your powers are against me, Mega-jerk!"  
**Megaman:** "I'm as good or better than you, Protoman!"

Also, without exception, every time Megaman absorbs a robot master's powers, either the enemy robot exclaims, "No! Now you have my weapon!" or Megaman himself says, "Now I have your weapon!"

Lastly, there was a line by a guy in a helicopter radioing for assistance, beginning with "This is Captain Lee!" and his voice sounded suspiciously like Stan Lee's. But would Lee really do a cameo for a Capcom animation? Unfortunately, I cannot find any evidence to support my crazy theory.
