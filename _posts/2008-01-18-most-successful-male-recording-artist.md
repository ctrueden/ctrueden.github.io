I saw a statistic today that Madonna is the most successful (as far as record sales go) female recording artist of all time, according to the Guinness Book of World Records (though [some say it's Celine Dion](http://www.chartattack.com/DAMN/2004/09/1303.cfm)). This led me to wonder: what about the most successful male recording artist?

Obviously, it's the Beatles, but they're not just one guy. So who's next?

Elvis Presley? Michael Jackson? MC Frontalot?

Nope, [none of the above](http://music.yahoo.com/read/news/51340235).
