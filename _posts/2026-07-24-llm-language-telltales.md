Have you noticed how LLMs (or maybe just Claude? IDK) love certain language constructions that humans rarely ever use?

* **reach**, **reach for**, **reach into**
  - "This second option is the one I’d actually reach for"
  - "most users should probably reach for `--python` mode first"
  - "The only route is reaching into `canvas._scene_canvas.scene`" ([source](https://github.com/napari/napari/issues/9250))

* **Happy to**
  - "Happy to test more ideas manually and report back"
  - "Happy to actually measure the crossover"
  - "Happy to prototype whichever direction maintainers prefer." ([source](https://github.com/napari/napari/issues/9250))

* **silently**
  - "Inspecting only the directly declared dependencies would silently miss that and select too low a JDK"
  - "we'd silently build against stale source"
  - "`matching_closure` still reports a hit and skips the rebuild — a never-tested version silently treated as passing"

* **quietly**
  - "He carries it quietly, the way he carries most things."
  - "the data model quietly forbids the *good* version of it"
  - "the deep, structural contentment of someone whose capacity for doubt had been quietly erased"
  - "`rm` as a top-level verb quietly re-frames cjdk as a JDK *manager* rather than a cache"

* **shape**
  - "Good — I have the shape of the API now."
  - "Yes, teach the Updater coordinates — here’s the shape"
  - "jgo v2 changes my calculus significantly — and one thing I found in it changes the shape of the answer"
  - "knowing the shape of something was the first step toward addressing it"
  - "knowing it changed the shape of his motivation in a way he could not entirely articulate"
  - "the only upstream-shaped alternative is *yet another bespoke overlay class*" ([source](https://github.com/napari/napari/issues/9250))
  - "Proposed API (sketch — for maintainers to shape)" ([source](https://github.com/napari/napari/issues/9250))

* **surface** as a verb
  - "Real differences the tool surfaced"
  - "reinstating it on this branch surfaced the flake"
  - "But it surfaced a *different*, related collision"
  - "Reviewing Jython’s engine surfaced a real SPI gap"
  - "Getting there surfaced one genuine design refinement"
  - "melt can stay as-is for now since its log isn't currently surfaced anywhere"
  - "Alternatively, this could be surfaced as a documented, opt-in config"

All of the above are real examples I've seen either posted online somewhere or in my own interactions with Claude.
